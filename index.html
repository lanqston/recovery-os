INSTALL ON IPHONE

After the portal is hosted, open it in Safari → tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen. It launches full-screen like an app and keeps an offline cache.

AUTOMATIC SCAN BRIDGE

The canonical state file is data/recovery-os.json. Approved scanner publishes update that one file, increment its revision, append history, verify the write, and this portal pulls the new revision automatically. Stock Explorer research never edits the canonical tracker.
